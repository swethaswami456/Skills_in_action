## What Most Dental Websites Get Wrong

Most dental clinic websites look identical:

* Stock smiling families
* Generic blue-and-white theme
* Boring service cards
* Basic appointment form

If you want something unique, consider:

### 1. Interactive Smile Assessment

Upload photo → AI-style analysis → suggested treatments.

### 2. Virtual Clinic Tour

360° walkthrough of the clinic.

### 3. Treatment Journey Maps

Show:

```
Consultation
      ↓
3D Scan
      ↓
Treatment Plan
      ↓
Procedure
      ↓
Follow-Up
```

### 4. Before & After Slider

Interactive comparison instead of static images.

### 5. Patient Storytelling

Use:

* Patient photo
* Problem
* Treatment
* Result

instead of just testimonials.

### 6. Apple-Style Design

Think:

* Black + White + Mint
* Large typography
* Full-screen imagery
* Smooth animations
* Minimal cards
